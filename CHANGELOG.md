# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-12

### Released
- Published **student-success-signal-hub** as the front door to the EdTech operations cluster.
- Packaged student risk scoring, momentum signals, intervention queues, and evidence views into one public repo.
- Reframed the project around actionable student support operations instead of passive retention reporting.

### Why this mattered
- Institutions can have plenty of student data while still being slow to act on emerging support risk.
- Dashboards often summarize outcomes after the fact rather than helping staff decide who needs attention now.
- This release made the repo meaningful to advising, student-success, and EdTech operations audiences.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-02-09

### Shipped
- Standardized the first model for attendance, LMS activity, assignment completion, support flags, and momentum scoring.
- Added the first intervention-oriented queue views for operational staff.

## [Prototype] - 2025-05-06

### Built
- Built the earliest prototype around student risk signals and outreach prioritization.
- Tested whether the system could move from retrospective reporting toward actionable case review.

## [Design Phase] - 2024-01-31

### Designed
- Chose a student-support operations framing over a generic academic analytics dashboard.
- Kept the design oriented around intervention timing, not just performance summaries.
- Treated explainability as necessary because staff need to understand why a learner is being surfaced.

## [Idea Origin] - 2023-04-06

### Observed
- The idea emerged from the gap between educational data abundance and intervention clarity.
- The missing product was a signal hub that helped people act before a student disappeared from the system.
